# SSC CGL 2026 — Digital Product Business Plan

Ye poora plan un logon ke liye hai jinhe iss field ka koi experience nahi hai. Har step simple bhasha me likha gaya hai. Bas upar se neeche follow karte jao.

---

## 1. Business idea ek line me

SSC CGL 2026 ke aspirants ke liye ek **digital study kit (PDF notes + mock test)** banao aur usse **Facebook** ke through online bech do. Product ek baar banega, baar-baar bikega. Koi stock, koi delivery, koi shipping nahi — sirf ek download link.

---

## 2. Ye exam kyun chuna (market kitna bada hai)

- SSC CGL 2026 me lagbhag **28.5 lakh candidates** appear kar rahe hain (source: testbook).
- **Notification: ~21 May 2026**, **Tier 1 Exam: 30 September – 30 October 2026**.
- Matlab abhi se product banao to prep season ke liye pura time hai.
- Ye India ka sabse popular graduate-level govt exam hai. FB pe iske lakhon active aspirants hain.

> Simple baat: itne bade audience me se agar sirf **500 log** bhi ₹149 ka product khareed lein, to woh **₹74,500** ho jaata hai — ek hi product se.

---

## 3. Kya banega (product)

Ek bundle "SSC CGL 2026 Starter Kit" jisme:

| Item | Kya hai |
|------|---------|
| Study Notes PDF | Exam pattern + har subject ke important topics + shortcuts |
| Full Mock Test | 100 questions (real pattern jaisa) with answer key |
| Current Affairs / GK capsule | Ready-to-revise points |
| Bonus: Study Plan | Roz kya padhna hai, ek simple timetable |

Ye saare materials is folder me main bana raha hoon. Aapko sirf inhe use karna hai.

---

## 4. Exam ka confirmed pattern (product isi pe based hai)

**Tier 1 (Computer Based):**
- 4 sections: General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension
- Har section: 25 questions × 2 marks = 50 marks
- Total: **100 questions / 200 marks**
- Time: **60 minutes**
- Negative marking: **0.50 marks** har galat answer par

*(Content licensing compliance ke liye rephrase kiya gaya. Source: careerpower, testbook, indianexpress.)*

## 6. Paise kaise lenge (payment)

Aapko koi mehenga setup nahi chahiye. Do aasaan options:

1. **UPI (sabse simple):** Apna UPI ID (jaise `naam@okaxis`) do. Buyer paisa bhejta hai, screenshot bhejta hai, aap download link de dete ho.
2. **Razorpay Payment Link (professional):** razorpay.com pe free account banao → "Payment Links" → ek link banao → wahi link landing page pe laga do. Paisa aate hi automatic ho jaata hai.

Shuruaat UPI se karo, jab sales aane lage to Razorpay pe shift ho jao.

## 7. Product kaha rakhna hai (delivery)

- Saari PDF ek **Google Drive** folder me daal do.
- Us folder ko "Anyone with the link can view" kar do.
- Ya ek single download link banao.
- Buyer ko payment ke baad ye link WhatsApp/Telegram pe bhej do (ya landing page pe payment ke baad dikhado).

---

## 8. Facebook selling — poora funnel (step by step)

Ye sabse important part hai. 4 simple charan:

**Charan 1 — Presence banao (Din 1-2)**
- Ek FB **Page** banao: naam jaise "SSC CGL Success Adda" ya "CGL Toppers Notes".
- Ek FB **Group** banao usi naam se. Group me log free tips ke liye judte hain.

**Charan 2 — Free se trust banao (Din 3-10)**
- Roz 1 post: ek GK question, ek shortcut, ek motivation line.
- Ek **free sample PDF** (main bana raha hoon `04_FACEBOOK_MARKETING_KIT` me) group me daalo. Log download karenge, trust banega.
- Har free PDF ke end me likho: "Full kit ke liye DM karo / link pe click karo".

**Charan 3 — Sell karo (Din 10 se aage)**
- Group + Page pe product post daalo (caption main de raha hoon).
- Landing page ka link har post me daalo.
- Interested logon ko DM me link + UPI/Razorpay bhejo.

**Charan 4 — Paid ads (optional, jab confidence aa jaye)**
- ₹100-200/din se FB ad chalao. Ad copy main de raha hoon.
- Target: age 18-30, interest "government jobs", "SSC", location India.
