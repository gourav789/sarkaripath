# PART A — VERBAL REASONING

## 1. Analogy (Similarity) ⭐ HIGH WEIGHTAGE (~3–4 Qs)

Analogy har exam me aata hai — sabse zyada weightage wala topic. Do cheezon ke beech ka rishta samajh kar wahi rishta doosri jodi me lagana.

**Sub-types:**
- **Semantic Analogy** — meaning-based (Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : School)
- **Symbolic/Number Analogy** — numbers ka relation (2:8 :: 3:27, cube relation)
- **Figural Analogy** — aakriti (shape/rotation) ka relation
- **Letter Analogy** — alphabet position based (AB : CD :: EF : ?)
- **GK-based Analogy** — Country:Capital, Author:Book, Instrument:Player

**Question types:** Choose the analogous pair / Find the missing term / Odd analogy.

**Strategy:** Pehle exact relationship define karo (cause-effect, part-whole, worker-tool, synonym-antonym, degree). Alphabet ki position (A=1…Z=26) aur reverse (A=26…Z=1) ratt lo. Squares/cubes tak 1–20 yaad rakho.

---

## 2. Classification (Odd One Out) ⭐ HIGH (~2–3 Qs)

Ek group me se woh element dhoondhna jo baaki se alag ho.

**Sub-types:** Semantic (meaning), Symbolic/Number, Figural, Letter-based.

**Question types:** 4 options me se odd one, ya odd group (letter-cluster) nikalna.

**Strategy:** Har option ke beech common property dhoondho — prime/composite, perfect square, same category (fruits, metals), vowel-consonant pattern. Number classification me divisibility aur square-cube pattern check karo.

---

## 3. Series ⭐ HIGH (~2–3 Qs)

Ek sequence me agla/missing term nikalna.

**Sub-types:**
- **Number Series** — AP, GP, squares, cubes, primes, mixed (×2+1, alternating)
- **Alphabet/Letter Series** — position shift, gap patterns
- **Alphanumeric Series** — mix of letters+numbers+symbols
- **Figural Series** — non-verbal, rotation/addition of elements
- **Correspondence / Continuous pattern series**

**Strategy:** Consecutive terms ka difference nikalo (first difference, second difference). Agar constant nahi to ratio dekho (GP). Squares/cubes aur prime series ko instantly pehchano.

---

## 4. Coding–Decoding ⭐ HIGH (~2–3 Qs)

Ek fictional code language ke rules samajhna aur apply karna.

**Sub-types:**
- **Letter Coding** (shift/reverse based)
- **Number Coding** (letter → number)
- **Symbol Coding**
- **Substitution Coding** (word ke badle doosra word)
- **Conditional / Matrix coding** (Tier 2 me common)
- **Chinese/Deceptive coding** (compare karke rule nikalna)

**Strategy:** Letters ko number me convert karke pattern dhoondho. Forward/backward shift note karo. Deceptive coding me do statements compare karke common word–code match karo.

---

## 5. Blood Relations (~1–2 Qs)

Family relationships ke problems.

**Sub-types:** Direct relation, Coded relation (symbols +, −, ×, ÷), Family tree/puzzle, Pointing/dialogue based.

**Strategy:** Hamesha family-tree diagram banao. Male ke liye +/□, female ke liye −/○ symbol fix karo. Generation-wise horizontal lines banao.

---

## 6. Direction Sense (~1 Q)

Movement aur final distance/direction nikalna.

**Sub-types:** Simple path, Left-right turns, Shadow-based (sunrise/sunset), Shortest distance (Pythagoras).

**Strategy:** North upar rakh kar hamesha diagram banao. Turns ko step-by-step draw karo. Straight-line distance ke liye Pythagoras theorem (a²+b²=c²) use karo.

---

## 7. Order & Ranking (~1 Q)

Positions/rank nikalna (from top/bottom/left/right).

**Formula yaad rakho:** Total = (position from left) + (position from right) − 1.

---

## 8. Syllogism (~1–2 Qs)

Statements diye jaate hain, unse valid conclusion nikalna.

**Approach:** Venn diagram banao. "All / Some / No" ke rules samjho. Possibility cases dhyan se check karo. "Some A are B" ⇒ "Some B are A" hamesha true.

---

## 9. Venn Diagram & Logical Sets (~1–2 Qs)

Do type: (a) diagram me relation match karna (Dog, Animal, Mammal), (b) counting-based DI Venn.

---

## 10. Statement & Conclusion / Assumption / Course of Action / Inference (~1–2 Qs)

Critical-thinking based. Diye gaye statement se kya definitely follow karta hai.

**Rule:** Sirf jo statement me diya hai uspe base karo — apna real-world knowledge mat lagao (jab tak universal truth na ho).

---

## 11. Arithmetic / Analytical Reasoning (~1 Q)

Word problems jisme basic maths + logic (age, coins, simple puzzles). Numerical Operations aur symbolic operations (BODMAS with fake symbols) yahan aate hain.

---

## 12. Word Building / Meaningful Word (~1 Q)

Diye gaye letters se meaningful word banana, ya kaunse letters bacheंge.

---

## 13. Missing Number / Matrix (~1–2 Qs)

3×3 ya diagram me numbers ke beech pattern dhoondh kar missing number nikalna. Har row/column/diagonal ka relation check karo.

---

## 14. Puzzles & Seating Arrangement (Tier 2 me zyada)

Linear/circular arrangement, floor puzzle, scheduling. Tier 1 me halka, Tier 2 me detailed.

# PART B — NON-VERBAL REASONING ⭐ (kul milakar ~4–6 Qs)

Ye figures/aakritiyon par based hai. Bina bhasha ke, sirf pattern dekhna.

## 15. Figural Analogy, Classification, Series
Shapes ka rotation, addition/deletion of elements, mirror-based transformation.

## 16. Mirror Image & Water Image (~1–2 Qs)
- **Mirror image:** vertical mirror — left↔right flip (b↔d).
- **Water image:** horizontal mirror — top↔bottom flip.
- Letters, numbers, aur clock ke liye rules ratt lo.

## 17. Paper Folding & Cutting / Punched Hole (~1 Q)
Kagaz fold karke punch kiya jaye to unfold karne par holes kaha aayenge — symmetry samjho.

## 18. Embedded Figures (~1 Q)
Ek complex figure me chhupi hui simple figure dhoondhna.

## 19. Completion of Figure / Pattern (~1 Q)
Adhoori aakriti ko logically complete karna.

## 20. Cubes & Dice (~1 Q)
Dice ke opposite faces, cube ko unfold/fold karna, painted cube counting.

## 21. Counting of Figures (~1 Q)
Diye gaye figure me total triangles/squares/lines ginn na — systematic counting.

## 22. Space Orientation & Visualization
3D visualization, direction of shapes after rotation. (Space Orientation SSC syllabus ka official word hai.)

## 30-day Reasoning mastery plan

**Days 1–10 (Foundation):** Analogy, Classification, Series, Coding-Decoding — ye 4 topics roz. Har topic ke 30 questions.

**Days 11–20 (Build-up):** Blood relation, Direction, Syllogism, Venn, Order-ranking, Missing number, Matrix. Saath me roz 15 min purane topics ka revision.

**Days 21–27 (Non-Verbal + weak areas):** Mirror/water image, Paper folding, Embedded, Cubes-dice, Counting figures. Clerical topics touch karo.

**Days 28–30 (Test mode):** Roz 1 full sectional test (25 Q, 20 min). Galtiyon ka analysis.

**Golden rules:**
1. Reasoning me **speed + accuracy dono** chahiye — target: 25 Q in 15–18 min.
2. Non-verbal ko kabhi mat chhodo — ye scoring hai aur log ignore karte hain.
3. Har galat question ka reason likho, ek "error notebook" banao.
4. PYQs (previous year questions) sabse important — pattern repeat hote hain.

---

## Recommended practice approach

- Roz kam se kam 40–50 reasoning questions.
- Har hafte 1 sectional mock (timed).
- Weak topics ki alag list banao aur unpe extra 20 questions roz.
